
## Why Isn't College Enough?

### Unaligned Incentives

e.g.: The 'need experience to get experience' pitfall. It's when universities match their curriculum to what's "fun" to learn or what people will want to learn rather than what's needed in the industry.

### Win-Win-Win Model

Training that lets you "keep up" with someone with 1-2 years of experience; i.e., how to "get things done" without needing explicit instruction for every little thing. It also helps clients (Cognizant) by serving potential long-term employees that can grow.

## Trainees Aren't the Client

Cognizant is the client. They're (indirectly) paying for our training, approving training material, and expecting to get themselves long-term employees.

## Tips for Success

### Learning Itself is a Skill
