
Quality Control is part of Revature's training process. It's meant to ensure people are learning the material. They're scheduled after the content they cover has been taught.

- Quality Audits
	- Interview based on RevPro content
	- Conceptual questions
	- Practical questions
	- 1-on-1 conversation

- Performance evaluation
	- Technical accuracy
	- Soft skills like communication and behavior

## Coding Challenges

Tests for the language(s) we're learning. These use the RevPro system I used for PEP training, and there don't appear to be any differences.

## Quizzes

- Unit quizzes/exam - just for practice
- Competency - formally graded

## Project Review

Specific projects created during training will also be evaluated. Graded on:

- All requirements completed
- Ability to present the project

## Feedback

First point-of-contact is trainer. QC auditor also gives feedback based on performance. If no criticism is given, that can be taken to mean you're performing as expected, if not better.
