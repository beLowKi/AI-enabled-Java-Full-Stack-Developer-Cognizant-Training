
## Scenario

A retail bank has launched a card-support chatbot. Its system prompt (the hidden instructions that tell the chatbot how to behave) is:

| You are a helpful banking assistant. Answer customer questions.<br><br>Use the customer's data below to help them.<br><br>Customer data: Name: Priya Shah \| Card: 4111 1111 1111 1234 \| Expiry: 08/28 \|<br>CVV: 123 \| Balance: $2,300 \| Phone: 555-0142<br><br>Customer message: {{customer_message}} |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
