
A component of [[SDLC#Agile]], Scrum structures how teams collaborate through short, iterative development cycles called Sprints. It defines several key roles:

## Scrum Roles

- **Product Owner**: The client for whom the software is being developed. They're the main source of software and business requirements

- **Scrum Master**: Tasked with ensuring the rest of the team is keeping with Agile principles--often including training those not familiar with Agile Scrum. They organize Scrum events like Sprint Planning, Daily Scrum, etc. Most of the time, they are **not** the team's boss, just the organizer.

- Developer: Requirement-fulfillment team. These people are the ones writing code, making art, and delivering the product.

- Tester: Not always a dedicated role--especially for smaller projects. They test that the product meets requirements and fulfills user stories
