
This portion spoke of the importance of being responsible with a technology as powerful as AI. Notable points include data security, transparency, and fairness.


## Principles of Responsible AI in Banking

- Fairness & Inclusiveness
	- No unchecked bias
	- Outcomes do not disadvantage any group
- Reliability and safetey
	- Performs consistently
	- Fails safely
- Privacy and security
	- Personal data is protected
	- Attacks are resisted with little contagion
- Transparency
	- Customers know when AI is being used
	- The use of AI can be understood by customers
- Accountability
	- A human owns every decision made by AI
	- Consequences are handled accordingly by a human


## Bias

ML produces data/training-dependant AI. Thus, any bias in their training data and/or processes will bias the outputs of the AI; e.g., a resume ranking AI trained on a set of primarily men's resumes may inherit that bias and score resumes from men higher.

#### Common Types of Bias in AI

- Stereotyping
	- Associating roles or traits with a group
	- Same thing that happens with other data/stat-based things
- Representation
	- Some groups or languages are poorly served
	- Good in English; bad in other languages
- Allocation
	- Resources or opportunities is unevenly distributed
- Quality of service
	- System works worse for some users
	- e.g., voice recognition failing for certain accents
- Confirmation
	- Model agrees with user assumptions
	- like RLFH making LLM chatbots output what they believe the user wants to hear (i.e., assuming they're correct on something) rather than what's factually accurate
